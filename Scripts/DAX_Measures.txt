
Total Revenue

Formula:
SUM(Sales[total_amount])

Purpose:
Calculates total revenue generated from all completed sales transactions.


Average Order Value

Formula:
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Transactions])

Purpose:
Calculates the average value per transaction.


Repeat Customer Rate

Formula:
DIVIDE([Repeat Customers], [Unique Customers])

Purpose:
Calculates the percentage of repeat customers.


Revenue per Customer

Formula:
DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Unique Customers])

Purpose:
Calculates average revenue generated by each unique customer.
