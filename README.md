# AI Insight Sprint™ Case Study #1

## Fashion E-commerce Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

### End-to-End Business Intelligence Portfolio Project using Microsoft Power BI

---

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed as part of the **AI Insight Sprint™ Framework**. The objective was to transform raw fashion e-commerce transactional data into executive-level insights that support strategic business decision-making.

The solution combines data preparation, data modelling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboard development, and executive reporting using Microsoft Power BI.

---

## Business Problem

Management needed a comprehensive Business Intelligence dashboard capable of answering critical business questions regarding:

- Revenue performance
- Customer behaviour
- Marketing effectiveness
- Executive decision support

The existing data was stored across multiple datasets and lacked meaningful analytical reporting.

---

## Business Objectives

The project was designed to:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyse revenue trends across markets
- Understand customer behaviour and loyalty
- Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness
- Support strategic decision-making using interactive dashboards

# Key Business Insights

- Revenue peaked in May before declining sharply in June.
- Germany and France generated the highest revenue.
- E-commerce outperformed the App Mobile sales channel.
- Customers aged 26–35 represented the highest revenue-generating segment.
- Customer repeat rate stood at 41.21%, indicating opportunities to strengthen customer loyalty.
- Marketing campaigns averaged 9 days, with Email being the most frequently used marketing channel.
