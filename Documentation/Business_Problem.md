# Business Problem

## Background

The fashion e-commerce industry generates large volumes of transactional, customer and marketing data every day. While this data contains valuable business intelligence, many organisations struggle to convert raw information into actionable insights that support executive decision-making.

Business leaders require timely, accurate and interactive reporting to monitor organisational performance, understand customer behaviour, evaluate marketing effectiveness and identify opportunities for growth.

Without a centralised Business Intelligence solution, management faces challenges such as:

- Limited visibility into overall business performance.
- Difficulty identifying revenue trends across time and markets.
- Inability to effectively monitor customer acquisition and retention.
- Limited understanding of marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Delayed decision-making due to fragmented reporting.

---

## Business Challenge

The organisation required an executive dashboard capable of integrating multiple business datasets into a single analytical solution that would provide:

- Revenue performance monitoring.
- Customer behaviour analysis.
- Marketing campaign evaluation.
- Executive-level business insights.
- Strategic recommendations based on data.

---

## Solution

Using the **AI Insight Sprint™ Framework**, this project developed a fully interactive Business Intelligence dashboard in Microsoft Power BI.

The solution integrates Sales, Customers, Products and Campaign datasets into a unified analytical model supported by DAX measures, Power Query transformations and interactive visualisations.

The dashboard enables management to:

- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyse revenue trends across different dimensions.
- Evaluate customer acquisition and retention.
- Assess marketing campaign performance.
- Support executive decision-making through data-driven recommendations.

---

## Expected Business Value

The completed dashboard provides decision-makers with:

- A single source of truth for business performance.
- Faster access to strategic business insights.
- Improved visibility into customer and revenue trends.
- Enhanced marketing performance evaluation.
- Better data-driven planning and decision-making.

---

## Project Outcome

The Fashion E-commerce Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard successfully transformed raw operational data into meaningful executive insights by combining modern Business Intelligence practices with interactive dashboard design.

This project demonstrates practical application of Power BI, DAX, Power Query and business storytelling to solve real-world analytical challenges.
