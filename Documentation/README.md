# AI Insight Sprint™ Case Study #1

## Fashion E-commerce Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard

### Transforming Raw Business Data into Actionable Executive Insights with Power BI

---

## Executive Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using the AI Insight Sprint™ Framework to analyse the performance of a fashion e-commerce business. The solution integrates revenue analysis, customer intelligence, marketing performance and executive decision support into a single interactive Power BI dashboard.

The project demonstrates how raw business data can be transformed into meaningful insights that support strategic decision-making, improve operational performance and identify opportunities for sustainable business growth.

---

## Business Problem

Modern e-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional, customer and marketing data. However, without effective analysis, decision-makers struggle to identify performance trends, customer behaviour patterns and marketing effectiveness.

The objective of this project was to develop an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that transforms raw operational data into actionable insights, enabling management to make informed, data-driven decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyse overall business revenue performance.
- Evaluate customer acquisition and retention.
- Measure marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Identify high-performing markets and customer segments.
- Provide strategic recommendations to support executive decision-making.

---

## Dataset Overview

The project uses four integrated datasets:

- Sales Dataset
- Customers Dataset
- Products Dataset
- Campaigns Dataset

These datasets were modelled using Power BI relationships to create a unified analytical solution.

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of key business performance indicators including revenue, transactions, average order value, unique customers, revenue per customer and repeat customer rate.

### Revenue Intelligence
Analyses revenue trends by month, sales channel, country, customer age group and discount performance.

### Customer Intelligence
Explores customer distribution, repeat purchasing behaviour, customer value and customer segmentation.

### Marketing Intelligence
Evaluates marketing campaign duration, campaign distribution across channels and promotional performance.

### Executive Insights
Summarises the key findings and provides strategic recommendations to improve business performance.

---

## Key Business Insights

- Revenue peaked during May before declining significantly in June.
- Germany and France generated the highest revenue.
- Customers aged 26–45 represented the most valuable customer segment.
- E-commerce generated higher revenue than the App Mobile channel.
- Most revenue was generated without discounts.
- Repeat customer rate indicates opportunities to improve customer retention.
- Marketing campaigns remained active for an average of approximately 10 days.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- Strengthen customer retention through loyalty programmes.
- Increase investment in high-performing marketing channels.
- Focus promotional activities on high-value customer segments.
- Expand marketing efforts within top-performing countries.
- Monitor revenue fluctuations to improve monthly sales consistency.
